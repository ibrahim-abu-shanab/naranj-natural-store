# Development imagery

## Final NARANJ logo

The supplied transparent PNG at `public/images/naranj-logo.png` (public URL: `/images/naranj-logo.png`) is connected to the public header alongside the existing wordmark. Its original aspect ratio and transparency are preserved. Organization structured data and default social metadata also reference this real asset.

The bundled files are **illustrative sample photos**, not photographs of NARANJ merchandise. Product seed records remain unpublished until an administrator replaces/checks them.

Images were obtained from Unsplash's free image pages and optimized locally to WebP. Review the [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license) and the source page when reusing or replacing an asset. No product endorsement is implied.
